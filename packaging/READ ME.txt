Combat Logs
===========

This folder is where the combat logger (thaumiel + combat logger) saves your battles.

  logger status.txt        whether the logger is ON for your game version
  2026-09-28\              one folder per day
    Battle 7\              one folder per battle the server settled, named after
                           its settlement file (endbattle_7.pb). Retried or quit
                           battles get none.
      combat-log.xlsx      opens in Excel or Google Sheets. Two tabs:
                           Breakdown - damage and Daze per character and per
                           ability, with each one's share.
                           Every hit - time, character, skill, damage, Daze,
                           crit, Energy, stats at that moment, active buffs,
                           Anomaly buildup, and the enemy's DEF, DEF reduction,
                           DMG RES, extra Daze taken and the team's debuffs on it.
      combat-log.json      every hit, for programs and AI tools.

The two files appear a few seconds after a battle ends. They are the ones to share.

Beside them are endbattle_7.pb and endbattle_7_loadout.json: the server's settlement
and your build for that battle. The site needs both.

Each battle folder also holds the raw data the two files are made from. It is hidden,
because it can contain account details: share the files above, not whole folders.
(To see it anyway: in File Explorer, View > Show > Hidden items.)

If "logger status.txt" says the logger is OFF, your game version is newer than the
logger. The game still works normally; install the update when one is out.

Turning damage logging off: put an empty file named damage-probe-disable.txt next to
remielle.exe. Delete it to turn logging back on.
